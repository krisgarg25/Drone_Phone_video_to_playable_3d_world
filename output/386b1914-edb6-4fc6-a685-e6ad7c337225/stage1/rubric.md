# Rubric — Academic SE-Lab Project Report (derived from academic-paper-expert core principles)

Scope: full | pass_score: 85 | max_loops: 2 | min_issues: 2 | min_instructions: 3

## Dimensions (equal weight)

1. **rigour** — Every claim must be supported by a citation or a measured number traceable to project records; no invented data.
2. **structure** — Required elements present: 3-4 objectives (1-2 lines each), 3-4 line impact statement, literature survey of >= 5 real papers, methodology with a clear diagram, work-done section; single H1, correct heading hierarchy, GFM tables.
3. **register** — Academic tone: objective, no colloquialisms, no absolute/emotional phrasing.
4. **citations** — All references real, complete, correctly attributed; in-text linkage to the survey where claims depend on prior art.
5. **contribution_separation** — Prior art vs. this project's contribution must be clearly distinguished (survey synthesis + work-done section).
