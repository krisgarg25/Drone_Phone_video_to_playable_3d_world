They cannot tell you a wall is finished, because they know nothing about where you stood: circling a wall and staring at it from the doorway look identical here.
How to get a perfect scan
1. Walk slow arcs. Never spin on the spot — nothing new gets seen.
2. Every surface needs to be seen from spots far enough apart: step left, step right, and change height once. Standing still and staring gives the maths nothing to work with, however long you hold it.
3. Stay an arm's length to about 3.5 m from what you are scanning. Views taken almost edge-on cannot be measured.
4. Good light and steady hands. A blurry frame is a wasted frame.
5. One face of a wardrobe is the whole job. You never need the back of a wall or the inside of a closed cupboard.
