They cannot tell you a wall is done: they know nothing about where you stood, so circling a wall and staring at it from the doorway look identical here. Use AR coverage scan for real per-surface coverage.
How to get a perfect scan
1. Walk slow arcs — never spin in place (the pill warns you).
2. Every surface needs 3 angles: strafe left, strafe right, and change height. Standing still and staring gives you nothing to triangulate.
3. Stay 1–3.5 m from what you are scanning; steep, glancing angles do not reconstruct.
4. Good light, steady hands — the sharpness meter is your friend.
