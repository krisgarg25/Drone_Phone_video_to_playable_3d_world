            Required schema and templates

            CSV requires exactly six columns. t_sec must strictly increase; t_sec + time_offset_s must be within the video duration. Latitude and longitude are WGS84 degrees; camera-centre altitude is ellipsoidal metres. horizontal_std_m and vertical_std_m are strictly positive GPS standard deviations in metres. Supply measured uncertainty, not assumed precision.

            Independent checkpoints: what Evaluate reads
            
              Evaluate optionally reads work/<scene>/survey/checkpoints.json. Checkpoints must be held out of alignment and pair independently surveyed references with corresponding reconstructed points, in the frames the evaluator requires.
              
            
              Required JSON fields: independent: true, alignment: "none", and coordinate_frame copied exactly from this scene's georeference.json, including its origin. checkpoints must hold 1–10,000 objects, each with reconstructed and reference three-number arrays in that shared ENU metre frame. No best-fit alignment is performed on these pairs.
              
            
              GPS samples and alignment inliers are not independent checkpoints. Without a validated checkpoint file, accuracy stays unknown. This console never creates reference measurements.
